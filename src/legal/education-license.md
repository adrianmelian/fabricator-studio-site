# EDUCATION LICENSE AGREEMENT

**Version 1.0, 13 October 2026. FabricatorStudio LLC, Westminster, Colorado.**

This Agreement is between FabricatorStudio LLC ("FabricatorStudio", "we", "us") and the
educational institution named on the order ("the Institution", "you"). It is the separate
written agreement for educational institutions that section 3.8 of the Studio Pipeline EULA
and section 3.8 of the Armature EULA refer to.

### 1. Plain summary

This summary is not the agreement. Sections 2 through 14 are.

**One price for the whole school: USD 1,000 a year.**

**Every enrolled student and every instructor is covered.** There is no seat count for
students and no charge to them. They can install FS Control Rig on their own computers as
well as the school's.

**Students keep it.** A student can go on using the versions they received for their own
non-commercial work, portfolio included, after the course ends and after this Agreement ends.

**Up to five instructors get a Pro seat** for the year.

**It is for teaching.** Production and client work need ordinary paid Seats.

**Nothing is switched off remotely, and everything anyone makes is theirs.**

### 2. Definitions

**"Term"** means twelve months from the date we receive payment, renewed under section 8.

**"Enrolled Student"** means a person enrolled in a course, class, or program the Institution
runs, for the period of that enrolment.

**"Instructor"** means a person who teaches, mentors, or prepares course material for the
Institution.

**"Authorised User"** means an Enrolled Student or an Instructor.

**"Covered Software"** means:

a. **FS Control Rig - Unreal**, including its module library and animation picker, in every
   version released during the Term;

b. **Armature**, for the institutional uses described in section 3.8 of the Armature EULA;

c. **the Studio Pipeline**, through the Instructor Seats in section 3.4 only.

**"Teaching Use"** means teaching, learning, coursework, assessment, demonstration, course
preparation, and a student's own portfolio work. Course material an Instructor produces for
teaching is Teaching Use.

**"Commercial Use"** means any use that is not Teaching Use, including production, client
work, or work for hire carried out by or for the Institution.

**"Your Output"** means anything an Authorised User or the Institution produces with the
Covered Software, including rigs, animation, scenes, exported files, and course material.

### 3. What you may do

Subject to this Agreement, we grant the Institution a non-exclusive, non-transferable license
for the Term to do the following.

**3.1 Install and use.** Install the Covered Software and use it for Teaching Use, on
computers the Institution controls and on Authorised Users' own computers.

**3.2 Distribute to your people.** Give FS Control Rig and Armature to Authorised Users,
including through a course platform, a shared drive, or a lab image, as long as only
Authorised Users can reach it. You may not post it publicly.

**3.3 Share rigs.** Give Authorised Users Control Rigs built with FS Control Rig and characters
prepared in Armature, for their coursework. A rig built with FS Control Rig needs the plugin
installed to open and animate, and sections 3.1 and 3.2 cover that.

**3.4 Instructor Seats.** Up to five named Instructors each receive a Pro Seat for the Term,
covering the Studio Pipeline and Armature's advanced tier, for Teaching Use. Name them by email
to licensing@fabricator.studio. You may move a Seat to a different Instructor when one leaves.
An Instructor who already holds a Seat of their own keeps it and does not use one of the five.

### 4. Students

**4.1 No charge.** Students pay nothing and need no Seat and no account to use FS Control Rig.

**4.2 After the course.** An Enrolled Student may keep using the versions of FS Control Rig
they received under this Agreement for their own non-commercial work, including building and
showing a portfolio, after their enrolment ends and after this Agreement ends, permanently.
Versions released after their enrolment ends are not included.

**4.3 Going professional.** Paid work or client work with FS Control Rig needs a Seat under
the Studio Pipeline EULA, the same as for anyone else.

**4.4 Armature.** A student's own use of Armature is governed by the Armature EULA, not by
this Agreement.

### 5. What you may not do

You may not:

a. use the Covered Software for Commercial Use under this Agreement; Commercial Use needs Seats

b. make the Covered Software available to anyone who is not an Authorised User, or sell,
   rent, sublicense, or include it in a product;

c. do anything section 13 of the Studio Pipeline EULA or section 7 of the Armature EULA
   prohibits, subject to the exceptions those sections state.

**We claim no ownership of Your Output and no rights in it.** Whoever made it owns it, as

**7.1 The fee.** USD 1,000 for each Term, for the whole Institution, whatever the number of
Authorised Users.

**7.2 Invoicing.** We invoice by email. The invoice is payable within thirty days.

**7.3 Taxes.** The fee does not include sales tax or a similar tax. If one applies, it is shown

**7.4 Purchase orders.** A purchase order is welcome for your records, but its terms do not
change this Agreement.

### 8. Term, renewal, and ending

**8.1 Start.** The Term starts on the date we receive payment and runs for twelve months.

**8.2 Renewal.** We will email a renewal invoice at least thirty days before the Term ends.
Paying it renews this Agreement for another twelve months. The renewal fee is the education
price published at fabricator.studio on the day we send the renewal invoice, and we will say
so in that email if it has changed. Nothing renews automatically and nothing is charged
without an invoice you pay.

**8.3 When it ends.** If the Term ends without renewal, or this Agreement is terminated, the
Institution stops installing and distributing the Covered Software, and Instructor Seats end.
Sections 4.2, 6, 9, 11, and 14 survive.

**8.4 Nothing remote.** We do not delete files, disable software, or reach into anyone's
computer when this Agreement ends. Rigs already built keep opening and animating. The Studio
Pipeline stops loading for an Instructor whose Seat has ended, as section 5 of the Studio
Pipeline EULA describes, and comes back if the Seat does.

**8.5 Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other materially breaches it
and does not fix the breach within thirty days of written notice.

### 9. Refund

will refund it in full, and this Agreement ends. This applies to the first Term only.
