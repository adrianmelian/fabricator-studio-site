### 5. What happens when a subscription ends

This section applies when a Seat lapses, is cancelled, is refunded, or is otherwise no longer
current.

**5.1 Nothing is deleted, and nothing is disabled remotely.** We do not delete your files, we
do not reach into your machine, and there is no remote kill switch in the Studio Pipeline. We
will not add one.

**5.2 Your existing work keeps working.** A rig built with the Studio Pipeline is made of
ordinary Maya nodes. It opens, it animates, it exports through Maya, and it does not ask us for
permission. Scenes, rigs, and files you have already produced are unaffected by a lapse,
permanently.

**5.3 The Substrate keeps working.** Every tool listed in section 3.6 remains available and
free.

**5.4 What stops.** The tools your Tier unlocked stop building and editing new work. That is
the whole of the effect.

**5.5 Your project configurations stay put.** They live outside the install tree and are not
removed by a lapse, an update, or an uninstall.

**9.1 The Studio Pipeline contacts our servers.** It communicates with FabricatorStudio servers

**9.2 What is transmitted.** Activation transmits your Account or session identifier, an

animation, exported files, project files, or any other content you create or load.**

**9.3 It keeps working when we cannot be reached.** The licence token carries a grace window of
**thirty days**, refreshed each time the Studio Pipeline successfully reaches our servers.
Within that window the Studio Pipeline continues to work normally with no network access at
all. A network fault, a firewall, an offline machine, or an outage on our side does not stop
your work.

**9.4 No remote disable.** We do not operate a remote disable, wipe, or kill switch, and we
will not build one. When a Seat ends, section 5 describes the entire effect, and it takes
effect by a licence token not being renewed rather than by anything reaching your machine.

**9.5 Restricted networks.** If you need to run the Studio Pipeline on a machine that cannot
reach the internet at all, contact us before purchasing. We do not currently issue offline
licence files, and we would rather tell you that before you pay than after.

### 10. The AI panel

The Studio Pipeline includes an optional AI panel. **It does nothing unless you supply your own
API key for a third-party model provider, in your own environment.**

When you use it, your prompts and the scene information the panel reads are sent **directly
from your machine to that provider**, under your own account with that provider and under that
provider's terms and privacy policy. **FabricatorStudio is not in that path**: we do not
receive, route, store, or see any of it, and we never receive your API key. We do not write
your API key to disk, to Maya preferences, or to any file.

The panel reads your scene; it does not modify it. Model output is generated text and may be
wrong. Section 18 applies to it in full, and you are responsible for what you do with it.
