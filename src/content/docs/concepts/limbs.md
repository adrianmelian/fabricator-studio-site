# Limbs

## What
Limbs are like templates, but instead of a full rig they are just parts, like an arm or a leg.

## Why
Limbs are the modular pieces of your rig. Add as many arms and legs as a character needs, or anything else you want to build onto it.

## How
Select a parent joint for the limb, then drag a limb component onto that joint in your Rig Outliner. The limb builds itself, joints and components included. Right-click joints in your rig to save them back out as a limb.
