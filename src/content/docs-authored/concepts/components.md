# Components

## What
The raw building blocks of a rig. Components give your joints life.

## Why
If you want more custom control over your rigs, use components and build them up into limbs.

## How
Components don't come with a skeleton. You bring your own and add components onto its joints. Each component has its own requirements for how it attaches, so hover over a component in the Palette for the details.
